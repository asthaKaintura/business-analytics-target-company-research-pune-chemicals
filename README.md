# Business Analytics Target Company Research – Pune Specialty Chemicals

## Project Overview
This project focuses on identifying and evaluating 25 SME-scale specialty chemical manufacturers with operational presence in Pune, India. The objective is to simulate a real-world industrial research workflow by filtering a large universe of companies into a high-quality, rule-compliant dataset.

The final dataset was developed using structured manual research, company verification, and a multi-criteria scoring framework.
---

## Objective
To identify and evaluate 25 genuine SME specialty chemical manufacturers based in or operationally connected to Pune, ensuring they meet strict constraints related to manufacturing capability, scale, and sector relevance.

---

## Why Pune?
I selected Pune due to its strong industrial ecosystem supported by MIDC zones such as Chakan, Bhosari, and Talegaon. The region hosts a dense cluster of chemical, pharmaceutical, and industrial manufacturing SMEs, making it suitable for specialty chemical ecosystem analysis.

---

## Evaluation Framework (Federer Score Model)
Each company was evaluated using six weighted criteria:

- C1: Manufacturing capability  
- C2: India-based operational presence  
- C3: Product differentiation / specialty focus  
- C4: Technical strength of decision-maker  
- C5: Sector growth potential  
- C6: Growth signals (expansion, exports, activity)

Each company was scored qualitatively as Weak / Moderate / Strong or even Weak-Moderate and converted into a weighted score.

---

## Data Collection Process
The dataset was built through manual research using:
- Google search queries related to specialty chemical manufacturers in Pune
- Official company websites for verification
- Public industrial directories and listings
- Cross-checking product portfolios and manufacturing claims
- Visited their linkdln profiles for more information and verification

Each company was individually verified for:
- Manufacturing presence
- Sector classification
- Operational relevance to Pune or surrounding MIDC regions

---

## Filtering Methodology
A multi-stage filtering process was applied:

1. Removal of traders, distributors, and service-only firms  
2. Retention of only specialty chemical manufacturers  
3. Verification of Pune operational presence (plant/R&D)  
4. Exclusion of companies above ₹500 Cr revenue (SME focus)  
5. Removal of inactive or low-information companies  

Approximately 70% of initially identified companies were filtered out, resulting in a ~30% yield.

## Key Insights
- The specialty chemical sector is highly fragmented with many SME players  
- Manufacturing verification is critical, as many firms appear similar but differ in actual capability  
- Pune’s chemical ecosystem is driven by MIDC-based SME manufacturers  
- Strict filtering significantly improves dataset quality and relevance
